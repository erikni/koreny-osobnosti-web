Czech playwright, dissident and statesman. He was the last president of Czechoslovakia and the first president of the Czech Republic.

In 2009, Václav Havel planted a Japanese flowering cherry in Prague Botanical Garden.

*Prunus serrulata* 'Amanogawa'
