Darwin’s orchid from Madagascar has an exceptionally long nectar spur. Night fragrance attracts a long-tongued hawkmoth whose existence Charles Darwin predicted.

Listed size: **epiphytic orchid**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **often December–January in European cultivation; white flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
