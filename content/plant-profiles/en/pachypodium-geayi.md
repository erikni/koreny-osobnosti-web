This Madagascan endemic has a swollen trunk and narrow leaves. It is adapted to a long dry season, and spines protect its young branches.

Listed size: **4–5 m in the wild**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **white flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
