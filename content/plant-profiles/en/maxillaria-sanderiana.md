Maxillaria sanderiana inhabits montane forests in Ecuador and Peru. It can grow on trees, rocks or leaf litter, and has single-leaved pseudobulbs.

Size and growth: medium-sized orchid; flower about 12.5 cm. Flowering: summer and autumn; fragrant flowers.
