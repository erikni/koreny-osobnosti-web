Red horse chestnut is a hybrid of common horse chestnut and red buckeye. It is deciduous, with palmately compound leaves and a dense rounded crown; its flowers are the main ornamental feature.

Size and growth: hybrid species about 9–12 m; cultivar may differ. Flowering: spring; pink-red flowers.
