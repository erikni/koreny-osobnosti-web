Antarctic beech has small deciduous leaves. Exposed conditions may produce a stunted shrub; in cultivation it usually forms an irregularly crowned tree.

Listed size: **tree or stunted shrub depending on site**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **inconspicuous flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
