The orchid recorded as Ada aurantiaca has conspicuous orange-red flowers. Flower spikes emerge between leaves on new growth and may arch towards their tips.

Size and growth: orchid; tree height does not apply. Flowering: orange-red flowers.

Lifespan: A numerical lifespan range is not established by the references used. These figures describe the taxon, rather than the current dimensions or health of the specimen in Prague.
