Camellia japonica is perennial and belongs to the Theaceae family. The NC State species profile lists a height of approximately 3–4 m.

The listed flowering season is spring, winter. Recorded flower colours include gold/yellow, pink, red/burgundy, variegated, white. Local climate and the weather in a particular year affect the timing and intensity of flowering.

Suitable light conditions listed in the profile are partial shade. Appropriate soil and moisture also matter; individual plants do not all reach the same size. Growth and appearance of the ‘April Snow’ cultivar may differ from the species; species dimensions above are not a precise cultivar measurement.
