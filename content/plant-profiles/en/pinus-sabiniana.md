A Californian pine with long grey-green needles in bundles of three. Its sparse crown and large, spine-tipped cones give it a distinctive appearance.

Listed size: **about 12–15 m, exceptionally taller**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **cones; no true flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
