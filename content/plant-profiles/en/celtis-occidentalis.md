Celtis occidentalis is deciduous and belongs to the Cannabaceae family. The NC State species profile lists a height of approximately 12.2–30.5 m.

The listed flowering season is spring. Recorded flower colours include gold/yellow, green, insignificant, orange, purple/lavender. Local climate and the weather in a particular year affect the timing and intensity of flowering.

Suitable light conditions listed in the profile are deep shade, full sun, partial shade. Appropriate soil and moisture also matter; individual plants do not all reach the same size. Lifespan: A numerical lifespan range is not established by the references used.
