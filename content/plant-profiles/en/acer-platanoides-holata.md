Acer platanoides is deciduous and belongs to the Sapindaceae family. The NC State species profile lists a height of approximately 12.2–15.2 m.

The listed flowering season is spring. Recorded flower colours include gold/yellow, green, insignificant. Local climate and the weather in a particular year affect the timing and intensity of flowering.

Suitable light conditions listed in the profile are full sun, partial shade. Appropriate soil and moisture also matter; individual plants do not all reach the same size. Growth and appearance of the ‘Holata’ cultivar may differ from the species; species dimensions above are not a precise cultivar measurement.
