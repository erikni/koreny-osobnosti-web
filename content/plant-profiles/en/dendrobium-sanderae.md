This orchid produces short, several-flowered inflorescences. Smithsonian Gardens describes 2–6 cm inflorescences carrying 3–12 white flowers.

Size and growth: orchid; upright canes. Flowering: white flowers with purple lines in the throat.

Lifespan: A numerical lifespan range is not established by the references used. These figures describe the taxon, rather than the current dimensions or health of the specimen in Prague.
