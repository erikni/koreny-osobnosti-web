This epiphytic orchid belongs to a group adapted to a marked dry season. Its flowers can have a strong raspberry-like fragrance; flowering is linked to a seasonal rest.

Listed size: **orchid with pendulous canes**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **flowers on older, often leafless canes**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
