Wollemi pine was discovered in Australia in 1994. This rare conifer is related to araucarias; cultivation helps build knowledge of a previously hidden species.

Listed size: **tree; smaller in cultivation than in the wild**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **cones; no true flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
