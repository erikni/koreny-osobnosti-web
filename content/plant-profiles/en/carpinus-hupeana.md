Carpinus hupeana is associated in specialist references with the variable Carpinus henryana complex. Collection identifications of its varieties are not uniform, so we retain the garden’s recorded name.

Size and growth: exact range for the garden designation is unconfirmed. Flowering: inconspicuous inflorescences; season unconfirmed for this record.

Lifespan: A numerical lifespan range is not established by the references used. These figures describe the taxon, rather than the current dimensions or health of the specimen in Prague.
