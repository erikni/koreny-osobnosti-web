Modern orchid references also use the name Lycaste virginalis. The species is important in Lycaste breeding, contributing flower size and form to hybrids.

Listed size: **orchid, not a tree**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **large white to pink flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.

Lifespan: A numerical lifespan range is not established by the references used.
