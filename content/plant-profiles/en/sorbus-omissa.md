This Czech endemic rowan was described in 2012. Small natural populations grow on rocky slopes along the left bank of the Vltava near Roztoky and Libčice; ripe fruit is orange to orange-red.

Size and growth: up to 12 m. Flowering: May.

Lifespan: A numerical lifespan range is not established by the references used. These figures describe the taxon, rather than the current dimensions or health of the specimen in Prague.
