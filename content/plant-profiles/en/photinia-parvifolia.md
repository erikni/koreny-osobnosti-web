A deciduous Chinese shrub with finely toothed leaves and dark red young shoots. Reddish to orange-red fruit follows flowering.

Listed size: **approximately 1,8–2,7 m**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **white flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
