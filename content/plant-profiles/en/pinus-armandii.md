Chinese white pine bears five needles per bundle. Native to China and neighbouring regions, it develops relatively large cones with thick scales.

Listed size: **medium-sized tree**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **cones; no true flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
