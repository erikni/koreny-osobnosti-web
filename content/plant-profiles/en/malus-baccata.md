A cold-hardy Asian crab apple with a rounded crown and small red or yellow fruit. Botanical collections include notably old specimens.

Listed size: **up to 15 m**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **spring; fragrant white flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
