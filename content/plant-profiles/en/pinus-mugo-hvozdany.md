Pinus mugo is perennial and belongs to the Pinaceae family. The NC State species profile lists a height of approximately 6.1–7.6 m.

As a gymnosperm, it does not produce true flowers or fruits like flowering broadleaved trees. Leaf or needle arrangement and reproductive structures help identify it.

Suitable light conditions listed in the profile are full sun. Appropriate soil and moisture also matter; individual plants do not all reach the same size. Growth and appearance of the ‘Hvožďany’ cultivar may differ from the species; species dimensions above are not a precise cultivar measurement.
