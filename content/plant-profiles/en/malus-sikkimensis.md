This Himalayan crab apple may also be shrubby. Hairy young shoots and red, somewhat pear-shaped fruit distinguish it.

Listed size: **up to 10 m**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **spring; white flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.
