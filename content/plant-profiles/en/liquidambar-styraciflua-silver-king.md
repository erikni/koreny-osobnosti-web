Liquidambar styraciflua is deciduous and belongs to the Altingiaceae family. The NC State species profile lists a height of approximately 18.3–30.5 m.

The listed flowering season is spring, summer. Recorded flower colours include gold/yellow, green, insignificant. Local climate and the weather in a particular year affect the timing and intensity of flowering.

Suitable light conditions listed in the profile are full sun, partial shade. Appropriate soil and moisture also matter; individual plants do not all reach the same size. Growth and appearance of the ‘Silver King’ cultivar may differ from the species; species dimensions above are not a precise cultivar measurement.
