Willow-leaved pear has narrow leaves that are initially silvery-hairy. Pendula has a weeping habit; a grafted specimen’s size also depends on its rootstock.

Listed size: **species approximately 4,5–7,5 m**. Flowering or reproductive structures: **April; white flowers**. These describe the taxon, rather than a measurement of the particular plant in Prague.

Lifespan: A numerical lifespan range is not established by the references used. Growth and appearance of the ‘Pendula’ cultivar may differ from the species; species dimensions above are not a precise cultivar measurement.
