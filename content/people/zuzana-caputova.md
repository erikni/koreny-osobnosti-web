highlights_en:
- Zuzana Čaputová (née Strapáková; born 21 June 1973) is a Slovak politician, lawyer,
  and environmental activist who served as the fifth president of Slovakia from 2019
  to 2024.
- Čaputová is the first woman to hold the presidency, as well as the youngest president
  in the history of Slovakia, elected at the age of 45.
- For this, Čaputová was awarded the 2016 Goldman Environmental Prize.
