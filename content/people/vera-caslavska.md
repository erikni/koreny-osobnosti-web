highlights_en:
- She won a total of 22 international titles between 1959 and 1968 including seven
  Olympic gold medals, four world titles and eleven European championships.
- Čáslavská is the most decorated Czech gymnast in history and is one of only three
  female gymnasts, along with the Soviet Larisa Latynina and American Simone Biles,
  to win the all-around gold medal at two  Olympics.
- She remains the only gymnast, male or female, to have won an Olympic gold medal
  in each individual event.
