- M., TGM nebo Prezident Osvoboditel (7. března 1850 Hodonín – 14. září 1937 Lány),

highlights_en:
- Tomáš Garrigue Masaryk (7 March 1850 – 14 September 1937) was a Czechoslovak statesman,
  political activist and philosopher who served as the first president of Czechoslovakia
  from 1918 to 1935.
- He is regarded as the founding father of Czechoslovakia.
- In 1918, Masaryk, along with his protégés Edvard Beneš and Milan Rastislav Štefánik,
  travelled to the United States to obtain support from President Woodrow Wilson and
  Secretary of State Robert Lansing.
