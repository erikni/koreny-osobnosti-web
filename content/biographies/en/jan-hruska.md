Jan Hruška (born 4 February 1975) is a former professional road bicycle racer from the Czech Republic, who turned professional in 1996.

His major results include two stage victories at the 2000 Giro d’Italia, a team time-trial victory at the 2003 Vuelta a España and the Czech national time-trial title in 1999. At the 2000 Giro he also wore the race leader’s jersey.
