Vendula Pizingerová (born Václava Horová; married Svobodová; born 18 February 1972) is a Czech philanthropist. She is the president of the Kapka naděje Foundation Fund.

She has received several Woman of the Year awards for her work, and in 2014 she was awarded the Order of Laurels. Her first husband was the composer Karel Svoboda.
