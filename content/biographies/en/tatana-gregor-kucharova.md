Taťána Kuchařová, formerly Taťána Gregor Brzobohatá (born 23 December 1987) is a Czech model and beauty queen who won the title of Miss Czech Republic and Miss World 2006.

In doing so, she made history to become the first woman from the Czech Republic ever to win the title at the final event of the Miss World competition, held on 30 September 2006, in Warsaw, Poland. She finished in second place in StarDance, the Czech version of Dancing with the Stars, in 2013.
