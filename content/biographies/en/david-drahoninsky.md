David Drahonínský (born 19 May 1982) is a Czech paralympic archer.

He is a double Paralympic champion, three-time European champion and World champion in individual and team events with Tereza Brandtlová and Karel Davídek. Drahonínský became a paraplegic after he fell from a balcony as a teenager.
