Václav Postránecký (8 September 1943 – 7 May 2019) was a Czech actor, director, theater teacher and dubber.

Alongside stage, film and television work, he was active in dubbing and taught at FAMU. From 2005 to 2011 he served as president of the Czech Actors’ Association.
