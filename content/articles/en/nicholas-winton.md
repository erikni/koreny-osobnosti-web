British humanitarian who helped rescue children from Czechoslovakia in 1939.

## Planting and plant

This entry is associated with **Morus nigra**.

**Year:** 2016.

**Planters or representatives (as documented):** Pupils of Kunžak Primary School; Winton received the tree as a gift.

## Clarification

The tree was presented to Winton on 28 October 2014 and planted in the garden by pupils in 2016. The gift and planting are separate events.
