Jindřich and Jan Pospíšil are Czechoslovak cycle-ball players and twenty-time world champions.

## Planting and plant

This entry is associated with **Fraxinus americana**.

**Year:** 2023.

**Planters or representatives (as documented):** Jan Pospíšil; the tree honours both brothers.
