Václav Cílek (born 11 May 1955) is a Czech geologist, climatologist, writer, philosopher, science popularizer and translator of Tao and Zen texts.

From 2004 to 2012 he directed the Institute of Geology of the Czech Academy of Sciences. His work brings together geological research, writing and the communication of science to a wider audience.
