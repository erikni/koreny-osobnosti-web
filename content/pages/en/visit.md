You can explore Kořeny osobností at Prague Botanical Garden in Troja. The project began in 2009, and its Trail of Personalities opened in 2017 to connect the plantings. Choose people whose stories interest you before your walk and discover the plants associated with them.

## Plan your walk

Start with the [catalogue of people and plantings](/en/people/). Search by name, category, year or plant, then open an entry. It gives the botanical name, the planting year where documented, and the planters or representatives. Follow the plant name to its characteristics and other related people.

To find your way around the grounds, use the Trail of Personalities map offered on the garden’s [project page](https://www.botanicka.cz/pro-navstevniky/navstevnicke-okruhy/koreny-osobnosti.html). Our catalogue does not provide exact coordinates for individual specimens; a plant detail helps you learn about it rather than serving as a navigation point.

## Entrances, maps, opening hours and admission

Before travelling, check the garden’s [entrances, transport information and site map](https://www.botanicka.cz/pro-navstevniky/zakladni-informace/kudy-do-zahrady-parkoviste-planky.html). Choose an entrance to suit your route; the visitor page includes a map of ticket offices and access routes.

Check [opening hours](https://www.botanicka.cz/pro-navstevniky/zakladni-informace/oteviraci-doba.html) and [current admission prices](https://www.botanicka.cz/pro-navstevniky/zakladni-informace/vstupne.html) directly with the garden. The outdoor exhibitions and Fata Morgana greenhouse have different schedules; the greenhouse is normally closed on Mondays. Check for holiday changes too. We link to current prices and seasonal hours instead of reproducing information that could become outdated.

## Questions about the Trail of Personalities

### Does the project include only trees?

No. The [plant catalogue](/en/plants/) also includes shrubs and orchids. The project’s first plantings were orchids in the Fata Morgana greenhouse, so consider a greenhouse visit when choosing which plants to explore.

### How do I find the plant associated with a particular person?

Search for their name in the [catalogue](/en/people/). For example, [Petra Kvitová’s entry](/en/athlete/petra-kvitova/) links to [Magnolia kobus](/en/plants/magnolia-kobus/). The botanical detail describes the plant’s characteristics and related plantings.

### Can families explore the project with children?

To choose a story with children, start with [Fictional and theatrical characters](/en/categories/pohadkove-a-divadelni-postavy/). These entries distinguish the character from the real planting participants.

### Does a name beside a plant mean that person planted it themselves?

Read the planters or representatives field on the entry. The person being honoured, a family representative and another participant are different roles. An undocumented planting year remains unconfirmed.
