The **Kořeny osobností** book records the first chapter of the joint project by Darina Miklovičová and Prague Botanical Garden. It was launched in June **2018** and covers the project's development from **2009 to 2017**.

## People and their plants

The publication introduces more than eighty prominent personalities and the plants associated with their plantings in the garden. It connects human stories with the botanical world and documents the beginnings of a project that continues with new plantings.

Explore later stories in our [people catalogue](/en/people/), read [about the project](/en/about/) or [plan your visit](/en/visit/).
